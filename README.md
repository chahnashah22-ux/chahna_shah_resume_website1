# Chahna Shah — Portfolio & Resume

Personal portfolio and editorial resume website for **Chahna Shah**, first-year business student pursuing an Integrated BBA–MBA at Nirma University alongside an online BBA in Digital Business & Entrepreneurship at IIM Bangalore.

## Features
- **Editorial Layout & Design Tokens**: 60-30-10 palette with typography (*Playfair Display*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*, *JetBrains Mono*).
- **Interactive Case Study Modal**: Deep dive into projects (Chaah Jewels, Social Mobility Documentary, Art & Design).
- **Interactive Filters**: Filter education and capabilities by focus area.
- **Print Optimization**: Clean print styling tailored for physical or PDF resume generation.
- **Zero Dependencies**: Self-contained static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript.

## Local Preview
To preview locally:
```bash
python3 -m http.server 3000
```
Then visit [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.
